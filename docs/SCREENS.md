# Mo-Tix screens

Captured on 42dot's official Pleos Connect emulator (v2.1.0, 2560 × 1440), with the car placed in Seongsu-dong, Seoul
(`adb emu geo fix 127.0557 37.5446`) and the gear and speed set through the Pleos Connect Virtual Console. The left
third of each capture is the Pleos Driving view; Mo-Tix is in the app area.

Showtimes, seats and payment are simulated (see the README). Film artwork belongs to its rights holders.

(Korean, the light display mode it ships with). It opens in cached mode on the saved Seoul lineup.
